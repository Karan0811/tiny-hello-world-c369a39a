# AI University Writing Style

## Tone

Professional

Friendly

Clear

Simple

Never arrogant.

---

## Language

Use simple English.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Explain every technical word.

---

## Paragraphs

Keep paragraphs short.

Maximum:

4–6 lines.

---

## Lists

Prefer tables.

Avoid giant bullet lists.

---

## Examples

Every concept should have:

- Simple example
- Practical example
- Production example

---

## Code

Explain important lines.

Do not explain obvious syntax.

---

## Difficulty Progression

Always follow:

Beginner

↓

Intermediate

↓

Advanced

↓

Production

---

## Avoid

Do not write:

"Trust me"

"Just remember"

"This is easy"

"Magic"

"Simply"

Explain everything instead.

---

## Formatting

Use consistent headings.

Keep spacing clean.

Use Markdown.

---

## Word Count

Target:

1200–1800 words.

Never add filler to reach the target.
